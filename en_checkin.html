Check-in Guide

Check-in is available from 3:00 PM (15:00).

Self Check-in Information

cocone house operates on a self check-in basis.
The room number and entry password will be sent via email once you have completed the Guest Registration Form.

How to Enter

  • 1. Please lift the cover of the digital door lock on your room door.
  • 2. Enter the password provided by the host [ex: 1234*].
    * The password is set to the last 4 digits of the phone number provided at the time of booking. (Separate instructions will be provided for Agoda guests.)

※ If you encounter any issues, please feel free to contact the host at any time.
Informing us of your estimated arrival time in advance will help ensure a smooth check-in process.
